MuscleClock

Muscle rest & PR tracker

A mobile app for tracking how long your muscles need to recover between sessions, and logging personal records for your own custom exercises. Train smarter — never overtrain a muscle or forget your best lift again.

⬇ Download for Android 🍎 Join iOS Beta (TestFlight)

Features

  • Muscle recovery tracking — log when you trained a muscle, set a rest window (with quick 48h/72h presets), and see a live status showing hours remaining or "Ready now!" when it's safe to train again
  • PR tracking — define your own exercises, log attempts with weight, and get notified automatically when you beat your personal best
  • Tags & filtering — organize muscles and exercises with custom tags shared across both screens, and filter in one tap
  • Stats dashboard — total muscles, total sessions, training days, currently-resting count, most and least trained muscle
  • Cloud sync — sign in with email; your data syncs across devices automatically via Firebase
  • No ads, no tracking — your fitness data stays in your account and is never shared
