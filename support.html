Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, tap the ⋮ menu in the top right, and choose Delete account. You'll be asked to confirm your password. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all your data. This cannot be undone.

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and follow the instructions sent to your email address.

My data isn't syncing across devices

Make sure you're signed in with the same email address on both devices. Data syncs automatically via Firebase — if it's still not showing, try signing out and back in.

Where is my data stored?

All your data is stored in Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud), linked to your account. Only your signed-in account can access it. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I use tags?

When adding or editing a muscle or exercise, you can assign it a tag (e.g. "Push", "Pull", "Legs"). Tags are shared across both the Muscles and PRs screens. Tap the tag dropdown at the top of either screen to filter by tag.

What does the status indicator mean?

Each muscle shows a live recovery status:

  • Ready now! (green) — the rest window has passed, this muscle is safe to train
  • Xh left (orange) — less than 6 hours remaining in the rest window
  • Xh left (red) — still deep in the rest window, more than 6 hours remaining

If you've never logged a training session for a muscle, it shows "Ready now!" by default.
