Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 28, 2026

MuscleClock ("the App") is an independent mobile application. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and how you can control or delete it.

What We Collect

Email address

Used solely to create and sign in to your account via Firebase Authentication. We do not use it for marketing or share it with anyone.

Fitness data you enter

Muscle names, notes, tags, rest hours, exercise names, and logged attempt weights you add in the App. This data is stored so the App can show your data back to you and sync it across your own devices. It is never shared or sold.

What we do NOT collect

We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or any analytics or tracking data. The App has no ads and no third-party analytics SDKs.

How Data Is Stored

Your account and app data are stored using Firebase Authentication and Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud services). Firestore security rules restrict every user's data so that only that signed-in account can read or write it — no other user and no public request can access it.

Third Parties

The only third party involved is Google Firebase, which provides the authentication and database infrastructure described above, under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone else.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the App:

Menu (⋮) → Delete account

This immediately and permanently deletes your muscles, exercises, logged attempts, and your account. This action cannot be undone.

You can also request deletion by emailing the contact address below.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised and the new version will be posted to this page. We may notify you via the email address associated with your account for material changes.
