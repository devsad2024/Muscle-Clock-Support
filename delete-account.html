Delete your MuscleClock account

How to delete your account and data for the MuscleClock app (iPhone and Android).

Option 1 — Delete it in the app (instant)

Open MuscleClock → tap the ⋮ menu (top right) → Delete account

You will be asked to enter your password to confirm. The deletion happens immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app (for example you lost your phone or forgot your password), email us from the address registered to your account:

Send to: guevmartinez@gmail.com

Subject: MuscleClock account deletion request

Please send it from the same email address you use to sign in to MuscleClock, so we can confirm the account is yours. We will delete the account and its data and confirm by reply within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (your email address and password record in Firebase Authentication)
  • All muscles you added, including their notes, tags, rest times, and training history
  • All exercises and logged attempts (your personal records)
  • Any recovery reminders scheduled on the device you delete from

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What is not deleted by us, and why

  • Your ad-free purchase. It belongs to your App Store or Google Play account and is recorded by Apple or Google, and by RevenueCat under an anonymous ID that is not linked to your email. This is kept for accounting and fraud prevention. Deleting your MuscleClock account does not refund or remove the purchase.
  • Advertising data. Data collected by Google AdMob in the free version is held by Google under Google's policies. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Routine backups. Copies in routine backups managed by our cloud provider may persist for a limited time before they are permanently overwritten.

See the Privacy Policy for full details of how data is handled.
